# ModQueue Copilot Video Script

## Target Length
90 to 120 seconds

## Script
ModQueue Copilot helps moderators make fast, defensible decisions without drowning in queue noise.

We start on the post card, where the moderator gets the verdict, confidence, top signals, and the fastest path into action.

When we open the full console, the first thing to notice is the layout. The sidebar can collapse with one click, so the moderator gets maximum room for the content instead of fighting a crowded dashboard.

In the Overview workspace, the main decision stays front and center. We surface the recommended action, confidence, risk level, and the exact reasons behind the call. The heavy supporting data is no longer dumped inline. Instead, analytics, policy comparisons, and the case file each open in focused popup dialogs. That means less scrolling, clearer hierarchy, and faster comprehension during a live moderation moment.

In Controls, the moderator can switch between setup, live post context, and reply workflow from the sidebar without stacking multiple dense panels on the same screen. The system can connect to a real Reddit post, compare moderation styles across policy profiles, and generate a reply draft aligned with the recommendation.

In Activity, every action is tracked as an audit trail. Moderators can review what happened, see the handoff summary, and maintain continuity across the team.

What makes this product strong is not just classification. It is the operational experience: clear decisions, compact statistics, explainable reasoning, and a workflow that feels built for real moderators under time pressure.

That is ModQueue Copilot: less noise, less scrolling, better moderation decisions.

## Shot List
1. Show the splash card and hover on the primary decision.
2. Open the full console.
3. Collapse and restore the sidebar using the arrow.
4. Pause on the decision hero in Overview.
5. Open the analytics dialog.
6. Open the policy comparison dialog.
7. Open the case file dialog.
8. Switch to Controls and show setup, post context, and reply flow.
9. Switch to Activity and highlight audit trail plus handoff.
10. End back on Overview with the recommendation visible.

## Demo Notes
- Keep the sidebar collapsed for most of the recording so the benefit is obvious.
- Do not scroll quickly; let the popup dialogs prove the reduced-clutter story.
- Emphasize explainability, moderation speed, and handoff continuity.
- If time is tight, skip deep reply editing and focus on the decision flow plus dialogs.
