# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

## Overview

ModQueue Copilot is a Devvit application for Reddit moderators. This Privacy Policy explains, at a high level, how the app handles information.

## Information Processed

To provide its moderation workflow features, the app may process:

- Reddit post information needed to analyze and display moderation context
- subreddit and moderation context needed to perform supported actions
- app state related to moderation activity, selected scenarios, policy profiles, and operational analytics

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate app features such as:

- analyzing posts and generating moderation recommendations
- displaying moderator-facing case files, activity history, and impact metrics
- supporting in-app workflows such as scenario loading, policy comparisons, and linked-post analysis

## Data Sharing

- ModQueue Copilot is not intended to sell personal data.
- Information is processed as part of the app's operation on Reddit's Developer Platform.
- Data may be handled through Reddit and Devvit platform services as required to deliver the app experience.

## Data Retention

Operational app state may be retained for as long as needed to support app functionality, moderation history, analytics, and reliability, unless deleted or changed by platform behavior or future app updates.

## User Control

- Moderators control whether they use the app in their workflow.
- Communities control whether the app is installed in their subreddit.

## Security

Reasonable efforts are made to keep app data handling limited to what is needed for app functionality, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the GitHub repository issues page for ModQueue Copilot.
